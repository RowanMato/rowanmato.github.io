### Resistance Disappearance
A lady at the resistance camp is worried about her friend. Someone she cared a lot about hasn't been seen in a while.
I later found this man, surrounded by some machines. One machine was screaming about revenge. After it attacked me, I killed it, and noticed that the man was dead. He had a journal on him, and it seems he came here to avenge his friends who were killed by this machine. The machine then, wanted revenge on him for killing its friends.
My companion, 9S, suggested we don't let the lady know what really happened to her friend. 2B said she deserves to know the truth.
After returning to the lady at the camp, I decided to tell her the truth. She was distraught, and we let her know that we put down the machine that killed her friend. She said that she didn't know what to do next. She had no one left to love, and nothing left to kill. 9S tried to comfort her by saying we'd come back and check on her. She didn't seem very talkative though, after that.
Later, returning to the camp after some other quests, I stopped to visit the lady, but she was gone. The other soldiers that knew her said that she left a while ago, and told them to give some things to us.
Some recovery items, a health upgrade, a bunch of rare minerals, and a lot of money. 9S exclaimed "What? All of this??" The soldier said the lady mentioned not needing it anymore.
I was left wondering if I should have just lied. Would it have been better to let this lady go on living with empty hope that her love would return someday?

### Photographs
A resistance member who's lost her memories, showed me three photos. I had to track down those locations shown in them, and return them to her. With each location discovered, memories would come back to her.
The photos were left by her love, after he died in a battle eight years prior.
After speaking to her post-quest, she mentioned "Honestly, I've been thinking a lot recently about whether it's worth it to continue living," but said that the data we recovered from the photographs tells her to continue on.
Despite this, she struggles to find a point to living in a world without her love.
In a later conversation, she reveals that she's started taking notes. Writing things down with the purpose of maintaining her memories, even when she forgets some of them. She notes being afraid at the idea of losing more memories, as it would eventually mean she's no longer herself.

### Food? (Check title)
One resistance member, in the forest camp, has an interest in food that humans used to eat. He wanted me to hunt some boar and moose meat for him, and then went on and on about various ways humans would prepare their food. He was completely fascinated by it. The only thing that paused his excited ramblings was the thought of what would happen to androids like us once the humans return to earth. With nothing left to fight, what would be the point of androids anymore?
He quickly brushed off the thought, and committed to practicing his food prep in order to offer the humans a feast once they arrive.
