## ✓* Spiritomb - It's Complicated…
![alt text](./Tangela.png)
> *Row 4, Column 3 ~ Shiny Spritomb, in Shield Version. Hatched in a Beast Ball.*

:::note
The full story of this hunt is 95% planning gone awry. To prevent this post from being even longer than it already is, this section will focus mainly on the actual hunt as it happened. If you're interested in the myriad of missteps that led up to this hunt, click through to read the full story [here](/posts/the-world-of-pokemon/108-malevolent-spirits/).
:::

Let me get this out of the way right up front. This hunt was originally supposed to be for wild Spiritomb in Scarlet Version. I was going to save near where one spawns, and reset until I saw it shine.
That is not what happened.

Instead, this hunt was a Masuda Method egg hunt in Shield Version.
I'm not a big fan of hunting via eggs (outside of Gen 2). I'd probably consider it my least favorite method of shiny hunting. So let that give you an idea of how awful the original plan for this hunt ended up being, that I eventually scrapped it entirely and pulled out the ol' Rotom Bike in defeat. Even if I hated doing it, I was determined to get this target one way or another.
